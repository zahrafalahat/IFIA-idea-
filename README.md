The project explores how artificial intelligence and wearable technology can support early awareness of emotional distress, improve parent-child communication, and promote healthier emotional development.

🔍 The Problem

Children may experience anxiety, emotional distress, stress, or behavioral difficulties that are not always easy for parents to recognize.

A behavior that appears to be disobedience or aggression may sometimes be associated with emotional distress or unmet needs.

At the same time, parental stress and exhaustion can make it more difficult to respond calmly and effectively.

How can technology help parents recognize these situations earlier and make more informed decisions?

This question inspired the development of ParentLink AI.

💡 Our Solution

ParentLink AI proposes an intelligent support system combining artificial intelligence, wearable sensors, and a mobile application.

The system aims to analyze relevant physiological signals and behavioral patterns to identify potential signs of emotional distress and provide parents with timely, context-aware guidance.

Rather than simply presenting data, ParentLink AI focuses on turning information into practical support for everyday parenting challenges.

✨ Key Features

1. Emotional Distress Awareness

Explore the use of physiological signals and behavioral patterns to identify potential indicators of anxiety, stress, or emotional escalation.

2. Wearable Technology Integration

A proposed smart watch or bracelet could collect relevant physiological data, such as heart rate, to support the system’s analysis.

3. Parent Stress Awareness

The system also considers the parent’s emotional state, recognizing that parental stress may influence parent-child interactions.

4. Real-Time Voice Guidance

The proposed system could provide short, actionable voice prompts during challenging moments.

Example:

“Your child may be experiencing anxiety. Try speaking calmly and giving them a moment.”

5. Personalized Parenting Support

Future versions could provide recommendations tailored to individual patterns, situations, and family needs.

⚙️ How It Works

The proposed workflow consists of five main stages:

1. Data Collection: Collect relevant physiological signals through compatible wearable devices.
2. Data Processing: Prepare and analyze the collected data.
3. AI Analysis: Explore patterns that may indicate emotional distress or escalating stress.
4. Contextual Guidance: Generate appropriate, understandable recommendations.
5. Parent Notification: Deliver guidance through a mobile application or voice interface.

This workflow describes the proposed system architecture. Its implementation and effectiveness require further development and validation.

🧩 Example Use Case

Imagine a child becoming increasingly distressed during a difficult situation.

ParentLink AI could potentially:

* Identify changes in relevant physiological signals.
* Assess whether the observed pattern warrants an alert.
* Notify the parent that the child may need emotional support.
* Suggest a calm, supportive response.

The objective is to help parents consider the child’s emotional needs before reacting to the behavior alone.
